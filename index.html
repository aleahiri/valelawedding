Our Story

✦
Your photo here

We met on a rainy autumn evening in a tiny bookshop in the West Village, both reaching for the last copy of the same novel. Vincent let Alexa have it — on the condition that she tell him how it ended over coffee.

Six years, two apartments, and one very spoiled golden retriever later, he asked her a different question on that same street corner. She said yes before he finished.

We can't wait to celebrate with the people who have shaped our lives.

— Alexa & Vincent
